And then I found out about funded challenges, and I started doing funded challenges. And at first I did okay, and I passed the challenge. But then once I was funded, I blew the account, and this set off a cycle of buying more challenges, blowing funded accounts, blowing challenges, and I found myself in what I came to learn was a major dopamine trap.

I ended up squandering tens of thousands of pounds, and it completely destroyed my life. People would come to me for advice on TA, and I'd give them it, and they'd come back and thank me because the advice that I gave them worked.

Yet here I was, almost out of money, consumed with guilt and shame, my wife was threatening to leave me, my children were asking me why they can't go on holiday this year, and inside I just wanted to die. I felt like I'd had my soul ripped out.

But then I came across Mark from Stoic Spartan Trading, and what really struck me was how genuine and authentic he was, but especially with his behavioral change background, and also his knowledge of trading. I'd never really seen anything like that on the Internet. So I contacted him, and within a few hours he'd responded.

We talked about the best package. I told him my story, and immediately Mark made me feel at ease. Instead of being filled with guilt, shame, regret, Mark started to fill me very, very quickly with pride, and in how courageous I'd been for reaching out, and he filled me full of hope, optimism, and a newfound resolve and determination.

He explained to me about the Stoic Spartan trifecta, and the holy trinity of trading. Unfortunately, I hadn't been aware of this before, and I just focused on the technical analysis part, completely neglected the trading psychology aspect. Once he started talking to me about the common flaws that many traders have, along with our neurological tendencies when faced with uncertainty, he started to help me slowly understand myself, and I realized that I'd been missing out on probably what was the biggest part of being a successful trader: self-mastery.

So I quickly got to work, and I found the whole process fascinating. The way Mark delivers extremely complicated concepts in the most simplest ways using analogies is just incredible, and I'll be eternally grateful for his teaching methods. It was quite a bit of work from myself, but like Mark says, it's probably the most priceless investment we could ever make in ourselves. And if we're truly serious about succeeding at trading, then we need to do everything we can.

So I followed what Mark said religiously. He implemented all the processes. We created rules together. We put in a risk management process in place, which I'd never truly focused on, and we started to fine-tune my strategy. We collected some data, and slowly but surely, my flaws were coming out less and less.

I found myself calmer under pressure. I found I had a plan, and I planned my trades, and I traded my plan. It's only been three months, but I've gone from blowing three or four funded challenges most days to now having the same funded account for two months, and I've already received two payouts and well on my way to get a third.

So it's safe to say I cannot recommend Stoic Spartan Services highly enough. I know it's only early days, but I genuinely feel like this was the missing piece for me, and I can't wait for the rest of my future and trading career to unfold.

My wife was at the point where she was saying she would leave me if I didn't give up trading. But after reaching out for help, she could see that I meant business and that I was taking action, and our marriage is being slowly repaired, which is no coincidence, as I'm no longer committing financial suicide on a daily basis. So the knock-on effect of this has been life-changing already.

I cannot thank you enough, Mark. You're the most genuine, enthusiastic, decent, and amazing teacher, mentor, and friend that I could ever wish for, and I already feel like I owe you my life. I am that grateful. Thanks again. Keep doing what you're doing. You're changing lives, mate.

And if anyone's wondering whether they should jump on board, take that next step, then please have no doubt. If you're experiencing any trading problems whatsoever, Stoic Spartan Trading does exactly what it says on the tin. You mastering yourself, and then you master the markets. And I feel like Mark has been walking on the path with me every step of the way since I reached out to him. Amazing. Thank you.”

A lifetime of reckless behaviour and addiction had brought me to the point where I had lost almost everything that truly mattered to me. Most painfully, I had lost my family. I no longer had access to my newborn son, and my eldest daughter wanted nothing to do with me. I had reached rock bottom.

One day I woke up and realised I couldn’t keep living that way anymore. In desperation, I reached out for help, and that was when I found Mark at Stoic Spartan.

Through Mark’s help, guidance and countless honest conversations, I started learning things about myself that I had never been willing or able to see before — the selfishness behind many of my actions, the ego that had driven so many bad decisions, and how often I blamed the world rather than looking at myself.

Before working with Mark, I could look in the mirror and genuinely hate the person looking back at me. Over time, he helped me completely change my perspective.

He taught me to let go of my ego, to accept the things I cannot change and focus on the things I can — starting with myself. He taught me how to respond rather than react, because my quick emotional reactions had caused so much damage throughout my life.

As my thinking began to change, so did my life. My loved ones started noticing the difference in me. Slowly but surely, relationships began to rebuild and my family came back into my life.

I found a good job, rebuilt my finances and started creating a life that once seemed impossible. But none of that compares with the most important thing: my children got back a loving and present father they could rely on.

Mark gave me somewhere I could talk openly about things I had carried for years — things I was ashamed of and things I felt I couldn’t tell anybody else. He never judged me. Instead, he helped me understand my resentments, challenge the way I saw situations and let go of burdens I had carried for far too long.

One thing Mark told me will stay with me forever:

‘You never really had a problem with drugs and alcohol. You had a problem living life sober.’

That hit me hard, but it changed everything.

Life still doesn’t always go to plan, but now I have the tools to deal with it. Mark taught me how to live differently — how to stop judging people, how to be kinder and more loving, how to stop allowing my ego to control me, and most importantly, how to start respecting and loving myself again.

When I reached out to Mark, I was desperate and dying inside. I had tried over and over to escape the cycle I was trapped in, but nothing had changed because I hadn’t understood that the real problem was the way I thought, reacted and behaved.

Mark helped me see that.

I owe an enormous amount of the man I am today to the work Mark did with me. My thinking changed, my behaviour changed, and ultimately my life changed. I am a better man because of it.”
