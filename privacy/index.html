Privacy

Privacy notice

How Stoic Spartan handles the personal information you share through this website.

Who we are

This website is run by Stoic Spartan (Mark). Contact: contact@stoicspartan.uk.

What we collect

If you apply for mentorship: your name, email address, the pathway you choose, your main goal, any message you add, and your confirmation that you understand the risk acknowledgement.

If you book a call: the details you enter in the booking calendar.

If you email us: your email address and whatever you choose to include.

How your application is sent

When you submit the application form, your answers are emailed to applications@stoicspartan.uk via FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a third-party form-delivery service that processes the submission in order to deliver it.

While you fill in the form, an unsent draft is saved only in your own browser (localStorage) so you do not lose your answers. It is cleared when you submit.

Booking a call

Free calls are booked through Cal.com (cal.com), which processes the booking details you enter.

Why we use it

To read and reply to your application or message, arrange calls, and provide the services you choose.

Your rights

Under UK data-protection law you can ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, or object to how it is used, by emailing contact@stoicspartan.uk.
