FAQ

Everything you should know before joining or applying.

Who is Stoic Spartan for?

Complete beginners, developing traders, traders pursuing funding, funded traders, profitable traders and advanced traders seeking further optimisation. The development pathway changes; the three core disciplines do not.

Do you provide trading signals?

No. Stoic Spartan is not a signal service and does not issue trade alerts or tell members what they should buy or sell. During Stoic Spartan Live, Mark may analyse markets and trade his own account in real time, explaining the market context, strategy, risk and decision-making behind what he is doing. Members make their own independent trading decisions. Any trading shown during a live session is educational and should not be treated as a recommendation or instruction to copy a trade. Trading involves substantial risk.

What markets are covered?

The principles apply across futures, indices, NASDAQ, S&P 500, gold, silver, forex and crypto, including Micro Nasdaq, Micro S&P 500, Micro Gold and Micro Silver futures. The exact technical focus can be adapted to the trader and instrument.

How are private sessions delivered?

Private sessions are delivered live on Zoom and recorded. The recording is sent back so you can revisit the detail at your own pace. Direct contact can continue between sessions as needed; the depth and cadence of ongoing review increases through Pro and Elite.

What is the difference between Stoic Spartan Live, Core, Pro and Elite?

Stoic Spartan Live is the group-access doorway into the Stoic Spartan environment: live market sessions, technical-analysis masterclasses, live Q&A, prop-firm development sessions, community and group trader-development education without private mentorship. Core is the entry point into private mentorship, adding personal 1-to-1 development across Technical Analysis, Risk Management and Trading Psychology alongside ongoing direct support. Pro is a more intensive private mentorship pathway, with weekly live 1-to-1 sessions, closer ongoing contact and deeper individual analysis and accountability. Elite is the most comprehensive and bespoke Stoic Spartan mentorship pathway. It is not reserved for advanced traders: the programme begins at the individual's current level and is built around the areas that will create the greatest progression — from Technical Analysis and strategy development to execution, Trading Psychology, Risk Management, processes and funded-trader development. Core, Pro and Elite all include the complete Stoic Spartan Live membership while mentorship is active.

What is the Performance Diagnostic Review?

A one-off 90-minute performance diagnostic review focused on environment, location, execution, risk, psychology and behaviour, followed by clear development priorities and an actionable plan.

Can I start in Core and move to Pro?

Yes. The pathway is deliberately progressive. A trader can begin at the level of support that fits now and move as their needs change.

How does the Elite application work?

Elite is not an automatic purchase. The application is intended to establish current level, objectives, trading process, performance constraints and whether high-touch mentorship is the right fit.

How is the Academy different from Mentorship?

The Academy is the educational resource ecosystem: free PDF guides and checklists today, with playbooks, short videos, research and tools being added over time. Mentorship adds direct analysis, coaching, accountability and individual development.

Are profits, funding or payouts guaranteed?

No. Trading involves substantial risk. Coaching can improve process, knowledge, decision-making and discipline, but no legitimate programme can guarantee a trading outcome.
