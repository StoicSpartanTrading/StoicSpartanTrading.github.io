What the four sessions cover

Four sessions. Each builds on the last.

Across four weeks, Mark works directly with the trader at their current technical level. That may mean starting with first principles and chart literacy, refining a developing strategy, or stress-testing and sharpening an existing edge.

Each session builds on the last so the trader leaves with clearer market understanding, stronger decision rules and a more repeatable framework they can continue developing independently.
