Mentorship & Performance Services

Choose the level of support that fits where you are now.

Choose the private or focused pathway that best matches your current stage — from ongoing mentorship and technical development to diagnostic review and bespoke optimisation. Stoic Spartan Live provides a separate group-access doorway for traders who want to learn inside the environment without private mentorship.

LIVE ZOOMPrivate sessions are delivered live, face to face.
RECORDED & SENT BACKSessions are recorded so you can revisit dense material at your own pace.
DIRECT CONTACTSupport does not end when the call ends. Contact between sessions can be frequent when needed.
BUILT AROUND YOUThe depth, cadence and focus adapt to your level, goals and current constraint.

The recording is not there because the teaching is unclear. It is there because serious development often covers a lot, and being able to revisit the session helps turn information into understanding and application. Pro and Elite remain the highest-intensity pathways because of the depth and cadence of ongoing review and optimisation.

Markets: support can be adapted across futures, indices, NASDAQ, S&P 500, gold, silver, forex and crypto, including Micro Nasdaq, Micro S&P 500, Micro Gold and Micro Silver futures, depending on the trader and instrument.

Private mentorship and focused development remain at the centre of Stoic Spartan. Stoic Spartan Live is the quieter group-access doorway. Core, Pro and Elite include Live access while mentorship is active; the Performance Diagnostic includes 30 days of Live and the Technical Analysis Masterclass includes Live throughout its four-week programme.

Elite

Application only

Fits · Any starting point · Bespoke development

The most comprehensive Stoic Spartan mentorship pathway, built around the individual rather than a predetermined starting level.

  • High-touch 1-to-1 mentorship
  • Complete trading-system development or refinement
  • Prop-Firm Scaling and Payout Rules masterclasses
  • Includes Stoic Spartan Live while active
See everything included

Best for: highly committed traders who want the most comprehensive level of individual mentorship and direct development available within Stoic Spartan.

Elite is not reserved for advanced traders. A trader may be taking their first steps or already be accomplished. The programme begins by establishing their current level, objectives, strengths, process and constraints, then builds the mentorship around the areas capable of creating the greatest progression — whether that is Technical Analysis, strategy development, execution, Trading Psychology, Risk Management, routines, decision-making, funded-account performance or a combination of them. The objective is to maximise meaningful progression from the trader’s actual starting point during the period of mentorship.

Everything included

  • High-touch 1-to-1 mentorship
  • Bespoke technical-analysis and strategy development
  • Trading Psychology and behavioural performance coaching
  • Risk Management, execution and process development
  • Complete trading-system development or refinement
  • Deep execution and behavioural review
  • Intensive accountability and individual development planning
  • Prop-Firm Scaling Masterclass — account scaling, consistency constraints, drawdown rules, risk allocation and funded-account progression
  • Payout Rules Masterclass — payout eligibility, withdrawal requirements, consistency rules and the risk considerations surrounding funded-account payouts
  • Priority support

Stoic Spartan Live access + Includes the complete Stoic Spartan Live membership while Elite mentorship is active — live market sessions, technical-analysis masterclasses, live Q&A, prop-firm development sessions, community and group trader-development education.
