Before you apply

Three questions people ask.

Do you provide trading signals?

No. Stoic Spartan is not a signal service and does not issue trade alerts or tell members what they should buy or sell. Members make their own independent trading decisions.

Are profits, funding or payouts guaranteed?

No. Trading involves substantial risk. Coaching can improve process, knowledge, decision-making and discipline, but no legitimate programme can guarantee a trading outcome.

I’m not sure which pathway fits. What should I choose?

Choose “Help me decide” and Mark will recommend a pathway based on your answers. You can also compare the options first.
